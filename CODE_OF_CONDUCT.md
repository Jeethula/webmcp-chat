# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and collaborate in good faith.
- Provide helpful, actionable feedback.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, abusive language, personal attacks, or discrimination.
- Trolling, insulting comments, and deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior through repository issues with the label conduct.
